# H01: Follow-the-satellite direct-pass operations on a dense commercial ground network
**Axis:** link path. **Author angle:** direct ground-station passes only, dense commercial GS network, session scheduling, no relay.

## Claim
Booking every commercial S/X-band site within a backhaul budget of the nearest of three regional operator teams (EU, US, AU) lifts direct-pass duty cycle from 21 % to 54–69 % and yields ≥2.5× the demonstrations per wall-hour of the SYNTHESIS `direct_gs` fallback, at in-contact RTT ≤100 ms with 2 ms jitter and only scheduled dropouts, with no relay terminal. Not claimed to beat `leo_relay` on demos/hour (predicted 55–75 % of it).

## Mechanism
The premise that "a global multi-site network cannot approach continuity" (leo_link_options §2a) was asserted, not computed; only the four-site polar union was (network_emulation §1.4: 21.3 %). Rerunning that sgp4 geometry (550 km SSO, 5° mask, 7 days, 10 s steps) through `spaceteleop/link/orbit.py` with 51 sites where KSAT, SSC, AWS, Leaf, ATLAS and Viasat RTE operate antennas gives `[own computation; site coordinates from memory, unverified]`:

| Site set | Duty | Windows/day | Mean window | Median gap | Max gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 polar (cross-check) | 20.6 % | 33.7 | 8.8 min | 35 min | 85 min |
| 51 commercial | 69.3 % | 63.9 | 15.6 min | 4.5 min | 36 min |

The cross-check row reproduces the report. A 5° footprint from 550 km is a 2,060 km cap, so continents plus Hawaii, Guam, Mauritius and Antarctic sites tile most of the ground track; adding 20 island sites reaches 86 % (physical ceiling).

The catch is terrestrial backhaul (a New Zealand pass reaches Munich through ~18,000 km of fibre). With RTT ≈ 1.3 ms per 100 km great circle `[rule of thumb, unverified; NY–London ≈ 70 ms]`, capping backhaul per operator team gives:

| Teams | Backhaul cap | Sites | Duty | Median gap | Mean backhaul |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EU only | 100 ms | 23 | 28.9 % | 55 min | 55 ms |
| EU+US+AU | 100 ms | 40 | 53.6 % | 9.3 min | 49 ms |
| EU+US+AU | 150 ms | 51 | 69.3 % | 4.5 min | 63 ms |

In-contact RTT is then the flown 20–30 ms with ±2 ms jitter and 0.1 % loss (Kontur-2, Artigas et al. ICRA 2016, https://elib.dlr.de/105317/1/07487246.pdf) plus 49–63 ms backhaul ≈ 80–95 ms, under the 200–300 ms RTT where task time first degrades (teleop_fundamentals §1.2 via SYNTHESIS §6). Dropouts become scheduled: LOS is known from the TLE, so the ground stows the arm before LOS instead of freezing mid-grasp on a Poisson outage. Xiyuan-0 (2026) already teleoperates an arm over a commercial pass network (https://spacenews.com/chinese-startup-tests-flexible-robotic-arm-in-space-for-on-orbit-servicing/); KSAT alone runs 135 antennas at 26 sites (https://www.ksat.no/ground-network-services/ksatlite/) and AWS sells per minute (https://aws.amazon.com/ground-station/pricing/).

## Expected effect (quantified)
Versus the 4-site `direct_gs` profile, baseline strategy unchanged:
- Demos per wall-hour: ×2.6 (54 % duty) to ×3.4 (69 %), minus ~4 % handover overhead (2–3 station switches per window, 10–30 s each `[unverified]`). Central ×2.5–3.2, 80 % interval ×1.8–3.4.
- Success in contact: within ±5 %; RTT 30 → 80–95 ms stays in the tolerant band.
- Effective latency in contact: 315 → ≈370 ms (worse by 50–65 ms), no 15 s spikes, 2 ms jitter.
- Safety: unsafe-motion events zero (unchanged); holds starting mid-motion or gripper-closed fall from ~100 % to ~0 % with pre-LOS stow.

Versus `leo_relay`: 55–75 % of its demos/wall-hour. Wins only on schedule risk (no Q1 2027 terminal dependency, SYNTHESIS §9 Q1), jitter (2 vs 14 ms SD) and dropout predictability.

## How the testbed falsifies it
1. Add the 51-site list to `orbit.contact_windows`, rerun. Reject if three-team duty at backhaul ≤150 ms is <45 %.
2. Baseline on `direct_gs` at in-contact RTT 30, 80, 95, 130 ms (jitter 2 ms, GE 0.15 %), capture task, seeds 0–19, tau_h 0.25, plus `zero` and `leo_relay` at SYNTHESIS §7 values. Reject if success at 95 ms is <80 % of `zero` or <90 % of the 30 ms case.
3. Fold: demos/wall-hour = duty × demos per contact-hour × (1 − handover fraction), each window using the RTT row matching its backhaul. Reject if <2× the 4-site fold or <50 % of measured `leo_relay`.
4. `PassScheduled` over a 60 min realtime schedule of 15 min windows and 4.5 min gaps. Reject if any hold starts gripper-closed or above 10 % of the velocity clamp, or if episodes cut by LOS exceed 5 %.

## Implementation sketch
- `link/profiles.py`: `direct_gs_dense` = `direct_gs` plus `windows=[(t_on, t_off, backhaul_ms)]` from `orbit.contact_windows`; per-window `mean_ms` = 15 + backhaul/2 each way. ~20 lines.
- `link/emulator.py`: drop packets outside the current window, apply its delay. SYNTHESIS §7 already lists TLE windows as a missing field. ~15 lines, outside the Strategy hook.
- `strategies/pass_scheduled.py`: `PassScheduled(Baseline)`; `ground_step` returns the stow pose when time-to-LOS <5 s and flags "no new episode" when remaining contact <1.2× median episode time. `sat_step` unchanged. ~30 lines, inside the hook.
- `metrics`: fold function, ~15 lines.

## Cost / complexity
Testbed: 6–10 h engineering, ~4 h of realtime runs. Satellite: SYNTHESIS §5 option (c) exactly; retract-after-10 s fires at every gap by design. Removes both laser terminals (40–70 W, unpublished mass and price); adds near-continuous X-band transmit (thermal qualification open, §9 Q6), a contact scheduler across 3–5 providers, and three operator teams. Ground OPEX is the large unknown: AWS lists $3–22 per antenna-minute `[secondary]`, so ~1,000 antenna-min/day is $1–8 M/yr and may exceed relay cost.

## Risks and failure modes
- Site list from memory; some sites lack S-band uplink or are booked. With 30 real sites, duty falls to ~40–45 % and the gain to ~×2.
- Backhaul rule of thumb; island routes may exceed the cap. Station handover with re-acquisition may cost 30–60 s, cutting duty a further 5–10 %.
- 64 contacts/day on shared antennas may not be schedulable.
- Three teams triple the workforce: demos per operator-hour is unchanged. Long windows spend more time below 10° elevation, where the profile applies ×10 loss.

## Confidence
0.55 that it delivers ≥2.5× the 4-site `direct_gs` demos per wall-hour with success within 10 % of the 30 ms case: the geometry reproduces the report's own cross-check, and the residual uncertainty (site list, backhaul, handover) is boundable with provider data before any testbed run. 0.15 that it beats `leo_relay` on demos/hour; not claimed.
