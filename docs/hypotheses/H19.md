# H19: Seed-and-replay: human demos on passes, onboard perturbed replays in the gaps
**Axis:** task and data design. **Author angle:** onboard replay/augmentation after a human demo (onboard-compute branch).

## Claim
On `direct_gs`, letting the satellite replay each successful human demonstration with perturbations during the 79 % of wall time with no link raises successful, ground-verified episodes per wall-hour by at least 2× over the baseline, with human-demo success unchanged and zero unsafe-motion events at pass/replay transitions.

## Mechanism
The pass regime is window-limited: 33.4 passes/day of 9.2 min, 35 min median gap, 21.3 % duty (SYNTHESIS §7). A trajectory the satellite has just executed is inside every envelope by construction; replaying it is the Orbital Express scripted-autonomy pattern (ground authority-to-proceed, then autonomous execution, https://boeing.mediaroom.com/2007-04-17-Boeing-Orbital-Express-Conducts-First-Autonomous-Spacecraft-to-Spacecraft-Fluid-and-Component-Transfer) and the Astrobee plans-through-LOS pattern.

That replays are worth training on is MimicGen's result (https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17596): ~200 human demos became >50 K by transforming demo segments into the frame of a newly placed object, replaying with action noise and keeping successes; policies on 200 generated demos from 10 human demos performed similarly to 200 human demos (§6.2, Fig. 4). On a real robot, 10 source demos gave 200 replays at generation success 82.3 % (Stack, 243 attempts) and 52.1 % (Coffee, 192 attempts) (§6.4); hard assemblies 8–36 % (Appendix P). Consistent actions beat noisy coverage (Belkhale 2023, data_pipeline §2.1). Flight tasks 1 and 3 (fixed cells) need no retargeting: every replayed scoop or pour is a new observation of the medium, which is the unique-value data (unique_data_study §5).

## Expected effect (quantified)
`direct_gs`, capture proxy, per arm:
- Baseline: ~13 successful human demos per 9.2-min pass at ~30 s and ~90 % success `[testbed-default estimate]` → ~18 successes per wall-hour.
- Hypothesis: 35-min gap at ~25 s per replay attempt → ~80 attempts; at 50–80 % replay success (MimicGen real-robot range) → 40–65 successful replays per gap, +56–90 per wall-hour. Total 4–6× baseline; ≥2× holds down to 25 % replay success.
- Replayed episodes: 0 ms link, 0 ms human; jerk and SAL within ±20 % of the seed.
- Bandwidth: replay logs 32 MB per pass for 4 arms (2.6 s of X-band); one 720p labelling stream ≈ 2 GB per pass, ≈160 s of a 9.2-min pass `[derived]`.
- `leo_relay`: +10–30 % `[unverified]`, from reset/swap time only; not the claim.

## How the testbed falsifies it
Profiles `direct_gs` (main) and `zero` (control: replay success with no link effect). Task capture, tau_h 0.25, seeds 0–29. Each successful human episode seeds 8 replays: object position perturbed U(±2 cm), drift and tumble resampled from the reset distribution, time-scale U(0.8, 1.2), setpoint noise σ 0.0015 rad; with and without object-frame retargeting. Pass schedule compressed to 55 s on / 205 s off (same 21 % duty). REJECT if any of: (a) replay success < 25 % with retargeting; (b) total successful episodes per wall-hour < 2× baseline; (c) any unsafe-motion event (setpoint change while `link_state` is hold, velocity above clamp at replay start or pass resume); (d) replay jerk or SAL worse than the seed by > 20 %; (e) pairwise joint-trajectory distance between replays of one seed below the operator noise floor, i.e. copies.

## Implementation sketch
- `strategies/replay.py` (~90 lines). `Seed(Baseline)`: `sat_step` also appends `(t, self.last)`, the sat-side applied trajectory. `Replay(Strategy)`: `sat_step(buf, now)` delegates to `Baseline` whenever `buf` is non-empty (ground keeps authority), else plays `traj[(now − t_start)·scale]` ramp-limited plus noise. Retargeting: MuJoCo FK of the seed's grasp-site path, rigid shift by the object-pose delta on the pre-grasp segment, DLS IK reusing `SyntheticOperator._dls` (~40 lines).
- `run.py` +20 lines: after a successful human episode, K `sat_episode` calls with `Replay(...)` and no ground loop; object pose from `sim.reset` stands in for onboard vision.
- `proto`: `F_REPLAY = 1 << 4`; `record`: `source`, `parent_episode`, `rtt_ms = 0`; `metrics`: `episodes_per_wall_hour` over the schedule clock. Controller and ground loop untouched; the hook fits.

## Cost / complexity
Engineering 8–14 h; runtime ≈ 1.9 h per condition (realtime). Satellite: v0 (stored 50 Hz joint trajectory, 42 kB per seed, time-scale, ramp) is deterministic and fits the rad-tolerant supervisor of SYNTHESIS §5; v1 retargeting needs one object pose per replay from the Jetson, so this is the **onboard-compute branch**, with playout on the supervisor so a Jetson SEFI only delays the next replay. Arm duty rises from 21 % toward 100 %: 4 arms at 30–80 W peak `[unverified]` against 175 W EOL (hardware_landscape §1); keep a `replay_duty` knob.

## Risks and failure modes
- Metric definition: replays are not human demonstrations. If only teleop demos count, the primary-metric gain is zero.
- Capture is the worst case for open-loop replay (MimicGen 8 % on gear assembly); a failed replay leaves the object at an unknown pose; in flight the next replay needs vision and reset is not free.
- 35 min of autonomous motion with no ground correction: cap replay velocity at 50 % of the clamp, require a per-pass authorisation flag, rely on the §5 envelopes.
- Power, thermal and Dynamixel life at 5× duty are unquantified. Labelling video takes ~30 % of pass downlink.

## Confidence
0.55 that `direct_gs` total successful episodes per wall-hour reach ≥ 2× baseline with zero unsafe motion. The multiplier is arithmetic given 79 % idle time; the uncertainty is replay success under perturbation on the capture proxy (MimicGen spans 8–82 %) and whether the program accepts replays as demonstrations (0.3 without an explicit decision).
