NO HYPOTHESIS: Post-hoc relabeling cannot move any collection-time primary metric, and the one thing it does move is too small on `leo_relay` to defend at >10 %. By construction, re-timing or replacing recorded actions after the fact changes nothing the operator experiences and nothing the arm physically does, so success rate, demos per hour, operator-loop latency and dropout safety are identical to the baseline; the overshoot-and-chase behaviour that latency induces (Rakita 2020, https://graphics.cs.wisc.edu/Papers/2020/RMG20/rmg-hri2020.pdf) is real arm motion that ends up in the satellite ground truth and cannot be edited out without fabricating trajectories. What relabeling can remove is the observation-to-action time skew inside each recorded row and the phantom actions logged while commands were lost or the arm was in hold. I quantified the skew against the current recorder (`spaceteleop/ground/loop.py` pairs the newest telemetry with the command just sent; the command takes effect after uplink plus the playout lag): about 37 ms on `zero`, 85 ms on `leo_relay` (18 ms down + ~17 ms half telemetry period + 30 ms up + 20 ms playout), 67 ms on `direct_gs`, 640 ms on `geo_relay`. The added skew on `leo_relay` versus the zero-latency baseline is therefore ≈48 ms, which at the task's cm/s object speeds and the operator's 7 cm/s approach speed is 1–3 mm of positional inconsistency against a 45 mm grasp envelope, i.e. under 10 % of the tolerance. The evidence that skew matters is real but sits at a different scale: UMI's inference-time latency matching ablation drops dynamic tossing from 105/120 to 69/120 when measured stream latencies are zeroed (https://arxiv.org/html/2402.10329), a fast task where hundreds of ms of gripper/robot misalignment decides the release; and no study collects demonstrations at controlled latency and reports downstream policy success (data_pipeline §2.3; SYNTHESIS §9 item 4). The only profile where relabeling plausibly buys >10 % is `geo_relay` (≈600 ms extra skew → 1–4 cm, comparable to the envelope), which SYNTHESIS §10 restricts to a control condition, and the falsification would require a behaviour-cloning trainer and zero-latency rollouts that the testbed does not contain (deps are mujoco, numpy, sgp4). The residual value of this angle is a recorder requirement, not a hypothesis, and it is already in SYNTHESIS §10 and data_pipeline §3.1: log the satellite-side applied setpoint and its `t_cmd_applied` per control cycle alongside the ground-side action, and drop nothing, so that either latency-matched deployment (UMI) or zero-skew re-pairing can be done offline by whoever trains on the data (≈40 lines in `spaceteleop/sat/controller.py` and `spaceteleop/record/__init__.py`, no Strategy involvement). I recommend the runner adopt that logging regardless of which hypotheses are implemented.
